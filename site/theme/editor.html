Theme editor

Nothing selected

Turn on "Click an element", then click anything on the screen. Its names, colours and sizes appear here. Changing one value changes only that element and property; changing a general colour (below) changes everything that points at it. Colours, font size, corners, borders and shadows are saved in theme.json; width, height, spacing and gaps in sizes.json.
